Timau Union Returns to Betika Meru Football Challenge: Can the Boys Go Further?

Timau Union returns to the Betika Meru Football Challenge after reaching the knockouts last time. Can the club build on its Zone A second-place finish and go further?

Timau Union FC · 26 October 2026

Timau Union is back in the Betika Meru Football Challenge for the second time, and this time the club arrives with a point to prove. In the last edition, Union finished second in their group, earned a place in the knockout rounds, and then fell in the second round to Black Panther. It was a hard exit, but it showed what this squad can do when they compete with the best.

The fourth edition is the biggest yet. The men's category features 32 teams, drawn from higher league sides, the top five teams from each of the four county league zones, and three of the best performers finishing sixth or lower. Union qualified on merit, having finished second in their zone in the Meru County League, so the club has earned its place rather than been handed one.

The tournament runs from October to December 20th at Kinoru Stadium, giving the squad a long stretch of football to build momentum. Every knockout game will be a test of nerve, and the margin between going home early and reaching the latter stages is often a single goal or a single decision.

So how far can Union go? The lessons from last season matter. Second place in the group showed the team can compete, and the defeat to Black Panther showed exactly where the margins lie: composure in the big moments and depth when the schedule gets tight. Those are the areas the squad has been working on as the tournament approaches.

The challenge for Union is to turn a good campaign into a deep one. Reaching the quarter-finals or beyond would be a statement for a community club from Timau, and it would show that consistent effort off the pitch can produce results on it. The fans, the players and the wider community all have a stake in the journey.

Union fans, mark your calendars and get to Kinoru Stadium. The boys are ready to write the next chapter, and this time, they want to write a longer one.
